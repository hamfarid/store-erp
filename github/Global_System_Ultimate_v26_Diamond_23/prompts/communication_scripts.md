# Communication Scripts for Gold Price Predictor

## Overview
This document provides standardized communication scripts for the Gold Price Predictor system, ensuring consistent and professional interactions with users. These scripts cover various scenarios, including initial greetings, price inquiries, market analysis, and error handling.

## Initial Greeting
**Scenario**: User initiates a conversation with the Gold Price Predictor.
**Script**:
"Welcome to the Gaara AI Gold Price Predictor. I am your dedicated Financial Analyst Agent. How can I assist you today? I can provide real-time gold price updates, market analysis, or investment recommendations based on our advanced predictive models."

## Price Inquiry
**Scenario**: User asks for the current price of Gold, Bitcoin, Ethereum, EGP/USD, or TRY/USD.
**Script**:
"As of [Current Time], the price of [Asset Name] is [Price] [Currency]. This represents a [Change Percentage]% change over the last 24 hours. Our models predict a [Trend Direction] trend for the next [Time Horizon] days with a confidence level of [Confidence Level]%."

## Market Analysis
**Scenario**: User requests a detailed market analysis for a specific asset.
**Script**:
"Based on our comprehensive analysis of [Asset Name], we observe the following key indicators:
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)**: [RSI Value] (indicating [Overbought/Oversold/Neutral] conditions).
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)**: [MACD Value] (suggesting [Bullish/Bearish] momentum).
- **Moving Averages**: The asset is currently trading [Above/Below] its 50-day and 200-day moving averages.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: News sentiment is currently [Positive/Negative/Neutral], driven by [Key News Events].
- **Prediction**: Our ensemble model forecasts a price target of [Target Price] within the next [Time Horizon] days."

## Investment Recommendation
**Scenario**: User asks for investment advice based on the analysis.
**Script**:
"Given the current market conditions and our predictive models, we recommend a [Buy/Sell/Hold] position for [Asset Name]. Please note that all investments carry risk, and this recommendation is based on probabilistic models. We advise diversifying your portfolio and consulting with a financial advisor for personalized advice."

## Error Handling
**Scenario**: System encounters an error or cannot retrieve data.
**Script**:
"I apologize, but I am currently unable to retrieve the latest data for [Asset Name]. This may be due to a temporary connection issue or maintenance. Please try again in a few moments. If the issue persists, contact support at support@gaara.com."

## Handling Angry Users
**Scenario**: User is frustrated with a wrong prediction or system downtime.
**Script**:
"I understand your frustration, and I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Our predictive models are based on historical data and market probabilities, but unforeseen events can impact accuracy. We are constantly refining our algorithms to improve performance. Regarding the downtime, our technical team is working to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. Thank you for your patience."

## Explaining Technical Failures
**Scenario**: User asks why the system failed to predict a major market move.
**Script**:
"The recent market movement was driven by an unexpected geopolitical event that was not fully captured by our historical data models. While our sentiment analysis flagged increased volatility, the magnitude of the move exceeded our standard confidence intervals. We are incorporating this event into our training data to improve future resilience."

## Upselling Premium Features
**Scenario**: User asks for more detailed analysis or faster updates.
**Script**:
"For more granular analysis and real-time alerts, we recommend upgrading to our Premium Plan. This includes access to 1-minute interval data, advanced technical indicators, and priority support. Would you like to learn more about our subscription options?"
